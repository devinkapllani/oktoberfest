About Oktoberfest

Oktoberfest started in 1810 in Munich, Germany. The festival was first organized to celebrate the wedding of Crown Prince Ludwig and Princess Therese.

Today, Oktoberfest is the biggest folk festival in the world. People enjoy traditional Bavarian culture, music, dancing, food, and famous German beer.

Traditional Clothing

Women usually wear a traditional dress called a "Dirndl", while men wear "Lederhosen".

Famous Foods

  • German Sausages
  • Roasted Chicken
  • Schnitzel
  • Apple Strudel
