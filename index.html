OKTOBERFEST

The World's Biggest Beer Festival

Oktoberfest is the most famous festival in Germany. It takes place every year in Munich and attracts millions of visitors from all over the world.

What is Oktoberfest?

Oktoberfest is a traditional German festival celebrated with music, food, dancing, traditional Bavarian clothes, fun rides, and famous German beer tents.

📍 Location

Munich, Germany

📅 Dates

Late September - Early October

👥 Visitors

Over 6 Million Every Year
